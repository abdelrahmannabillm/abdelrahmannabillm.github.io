AdventureWorks — Sales & Customer Analytics

Multi-page Power BI report · Star schema · 2020–2022 sales

Power BIDAXPower QueryStar Schema

A multinational bicycle & sports-accessory retailer dataset. Built a star schema from CSVs (Calendar, Customers, Products, Categories/Subcategories, Territories, Sales 2020–2022, Returns) and delivered a multi-page Power BI report with Executive Summary, Map view, Product Detail, Customer Detail, Decomposition Tree, and Key Influencers pages.

Core KPIs

  • Revenue / Total Sales (YoY %, MoM %)
  • Total Profit & Profit Margin %
  • Total Orders / Quantity Sold
  • Total Returns & Return Rate %
  • Avg Revenue per Customer
  • Top / Bottom Products
  • Sales by Territory / Country (map)
  • 10-day rolling avg / 90-day MA
  • Order target vs. actual

Business Goal

Help executives, sales managers, and product teams understand what is driving revenue and profit so they can:

  • Hit monthly orders target — track actual vs. plan
  • Grow revenue and protect margin
  • Reduce returns by surfacing problem products/regions
  • Expand in the right markets
  • Increase customer value

Maven Market — Retail Performance Dashboard

Power BI report · Star schema · 1997–1998 transactions

Power BIDAXPower QueryRetail Analytics

A multinational grocery / retail chain dataset covering 1997–1998. Built a star schema from Calendar, Customers, Products, Stores, Regions, Transactions (1997 & 1998), and Returns — delivered as a Power BI report with Topline Performance and Geographic / Store-level pages.

Core KPIs

  • Total Transactions & Quantity Sold
  • Total Revenue, Cost, Profit, Margin %
  • Total Returns & Return Rate %
  • Weekend Transactions / % of total
  • Revenue & Profit YoY (1998 vs 1997)
  • Avg Retail Price & Transactions per Customer
  • Sales by Country / Region / Store
  • Sales by Brand / Category / Department
  • Top Stores / Top Brands

Business Goal

Give Maven Market's leadership a single source of truth for performance across countries, stores, brands, and time, so they can:

  • Compare 1998 vs. 1997 and explain what changed
  • Identify most/least profitable stores & brands
  • Improve margin by analyzing cost vs. retail price
  • Reduce returns and operational losses
  • Optimize staffing & promotions (weekday vs. weekend)
  • Understand the customer base & basket size
